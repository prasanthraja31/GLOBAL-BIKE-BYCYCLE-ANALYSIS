# GLOBAL-BIKE-BYCYCLE-ANALYSIS
  An interactive dashboard that analyzes sales, profit, customer behavior, product performance, and overall retail trends for data-driven decision-making.
# ABOUT
Global Bike & Bicycle Analytics is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze bike and bicycle sales performance across different products, customers, states, territories, and customer segments. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of revenue, orders, product performance, customer behavior, and regional sales trends to support data-driven business decisions.
# TOOLS
Power BI – Dashboard development and interactive visualizations
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX – Measures, calculations, KPIs, and customer segmentation
Excel / CSV – Data preparation and source data handling
Data Visualization – Charts, maps, KPIs, tables, and slicers
# KEY INSIGHTS
Total Revenue: The business generated approximately $8.58M in total revenue.
Orders: The dashboard records approximately 1.61M orders.
Top Product: Trek Slash 8 27.5 - 2016 generated the highest product-wise revenue at approximately $616K.
Customer Segmentation: Champions contributed the highest segment revenue, followed by Loyal Customers and Potential Loyalists.
Regional Performance: New York (NY) recorded the highest order count among the displayed states.
Customer Analysis: Customer-level spending analysis helps identify high-value customers and understand purchasing behavior.
Monthly Trends: Sales vary across months, with noticeable peaks during the early and middle parts of the year.
Geographic Analysis: The dashboard uses state-level revenue mapping to visualize regional sales performance.
